image. Every story on the page happened, or was first reported, on
26 November 2026.

## The day's news

- **Lead — Russia's own figures: nearly 500,000 dead in Ukraine.**
  Moscow's own data puts its war dead near half a million, as reported
  by the BBC on 26 November 2026. It is the day's most consequential
  story: a toll the Kremlin's own figures now admit.
- **Oil shock.** Brent crude tops $94 after the Houthis claim a strike
  on Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura refinery, the kingdom's largest, and
  Riyadh says it shot down four drones over the site.
- **Trade.** Washington prepares to unveil new tariffs on 16 trading
  partners after a probe into excess factory capacity.
- **Sudan.** Drone strikes kill 19 civilians in Blue Nile state.
- **India remembers 26/11.** Eighteen years since the Mumbai attacks,
  marked on Constitution Day.
- **Colombia.** Police storm a supermarket and free 20 people held
  hostage for 17 hours.
- **Bangladesh.** The minimum wage for garment workers rises 36%.
- **North Korea.** Pyongyang orders Switzerland to close its newly
  opened diplomatic office.
- **Space.** The Hera spacecraft reveals close-up images of the
  asteroid deliberately hit to test planetary defence.

## How it was made

The front page was generated fresh in the build session for this
bounty and saved as `entry.png` (1024×1024, JPEG bytes in a file
named `entry.png`, as the rules allow). `main.py` (Python 3.13,
standard library only, no network) copies that file to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`, so both graded runs write the exact
same bytes.
